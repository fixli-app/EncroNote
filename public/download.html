Install on Android

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file and review Android’s install prompt.
  3. If asked, allow your browser to install apps, then return to the APK.

Android may show a warning because this app is installed directly rather than through Google Play. Only install an APK from the official site you trust.

App security & privacy

Notes are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. Your master password is needed to unlock the vault. EncroNote has no account, notes server, or cloud sync.

The Android app locks after one minute without activity. If the configured wrong-password limit is reached, the local vault is deleted. That limit does not slow guesses against a stolen copy of the encrypted vault.

No app can promise absolute protection. A weak password may be guessed offline, and a compromised phone or a device observed while the vault is open may expose notes.
